# English article for seohouse.agency — "How much does SEO cost in Saudi Arabia?"

SEO House is an SEO / web design / e-commerce agency (since 2017, office in Alexandria, Egypt) serving
companies in Saudi Arabia, the UAE and Egypt remotely. This is an **original English article** for the
English blog (not a translation): it targets people in Saudi Arabia searching in English, e.g.
"SEO cost in Saudi Arabia", "SEO pricing Saudi Arabia", "SEO packages KSA", "how much does SEO cost
in Riyadh". Search intent: a business owner/marketing manager budgeting for SEO and comparing options.

## Facts you may use (and only these as SEO House facts)
- SEO House does not sell fixed packages; the price follows the scope. Working range: **SAR 1,500 – 7,000**;
  the final price is set after reviewing the site and agreeing the scope; no number is committed before that
  review. Whether a price is monthly or a one-time fee depends on the type of work (an audit or build project
  is priced differently from ongoing work) and is stated in the written proposal.
- Six factors that set where a project falls in the range: site condition; number of pages or products;
  market and competition; scope of implementation (audit only / full implementation / implementation +
  monthly follow-up); content and development (in SEO House's scope or the client's team); platform
  (WordPress, Shopify, Salla, Zid, WooCommerce customization limits).
- Raises cost: large catalog/page count; strong competition for commercial keywords; more new content;
  accumulated technical issues to fix first; more than one market or language.
  Lowers cost: technically sound modern site; defined scope starting with fewer pages; in-house team handling
  changes/content; platform allowing changes without custom development; one clear goal.
- Before any commitment: free initial site review, free 30-minute call, then a written scope and price
  (what's included and what isn't). If the work isn't right for the client now, SEO House says so.
- Work in Saudi Arabia: demand analysis, competitor comparison, priorities by closeness to an inquiry/order,
  technical SEO, on-page SEO, SEO content, off-page SEO, measurement (Search Console, Google Analytics).
- One Saudi result you may cite: an online store in the Saudi market grew organic search revenue by
  **+432%** (SAR 19,956 → SAR 106,274 vs. the previous period, source: Google Analytics 4).

Do **not** invent other prices, market averages, competitor prices, statistics, guarantees, client names or
timelines. General, well-known SEO knowledge is fine (how pricing models differ — monthly retainer, project,
hourly; freelancer vs agency vs in-house; why guaranteed rankings are a red flag; what a proposal should
list; Arabic + English search in Saudi Arabia; local search in Riyadh/Jeddah/Dammam; Salla and Zid stores).
